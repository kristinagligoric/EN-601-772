Code of Conduct

The strength of the university depends on academic and personal integrity.

Course Conduct This is a class focused on controversial topics. All students are expected to respect everyone's perspective and input and to contribute towards creating a welcoming and inclusive climate. We the instructors will strive to make this classroom an inclusive space for all students, and we welcome feedback on ways to improve.

Academic Integrity Code This course will have a zero-tolerance philosophy regarding plagiarism or other forms of cheating, and incidents of academic dishonesty will be reported. A student who has doubts about how the Honor Code applies to this course should obtain specific guidance from the course instructor before submitting the respective assignment.

AI use We adopt the school-wide AI use policy. Relying on AI in place of your own reading, reasoning, and writing is likely to undermine your learning, and performance in the class. We encourage exploring the use AI tools for brainstorming, clarification, or editing, as long as you are mindful of how this affects your own thinking and understanding.

Discrimination and Harrassment Johns Hopkins University is committed to equal opportunity for its faculty, staff, and students. To that end, the university does not discriminate on the basis of sex, gender, marital status, pregnancy, race, color, ethnicity, national origin, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, veteran status, military status, immigration status or other legally protected characteristic. The University's Discrimination and Harassment Policy and Procedures provides information on how to report or file a complaint of discrimination or harassment based on any of the protected statuses listed in the earlier sentence, and the University's prompt and equitable response to such complaints.
